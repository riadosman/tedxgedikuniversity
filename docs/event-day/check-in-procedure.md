# Entrance check-in procedure

This scanner requires an online server decision. Camera decoding alone never authorizes admission. Use the [runbook](runbook.md) and [device rehearsal](device-rehearsal.md).

1. **Log in:** use your individual account at `/accounts/login/`, password and authenticator. The organizer assigns Check-in Staff, Event Admin or Organizer for this event. Never share accounts or recovery codes. If a session expires with an unresolved scan, sign in in another tab before retrying.
2. **Open the scanner:** choose Entrance check-in. Confirm event, device label, mode and freshness of totals. Actual doors time is separate from team setup. Only the organizer changes entrance mode, with recent MFA and a reason.
3. **Scan:** allow the camera over HTTPS, hold the QR steady and wait for the server. Alternatively use the credential input with a USB scanner. Select Next attendee after each result.
4. **Interpret:** VALID means a new admission was committed and the ticket is CHECKED_IN. ALREADY_CHECKED_IN means do not admit again; review the previous time shown to staff. INVALID, WRONG EVENT, REVOKED, CANCELLED, EXPIRED, PAYMENT REQUIRED, POLICY REVIEW or CAPACITY REVIEW means do not admit; consult the supervisor. Cancellation also revokes the old credential. ENTRY PAUSED/CLOSED means follow the supervisor's instructions. Email verification is not an admission requirement. No ordinary role bypasses these checks.
5. **Unknown result:** do not admit. Restore connectivity and Retry the same request. The durable receipt returns the original committed result. Refreshing/closing loses the browser's in-memory retry ID. A new scan still cannot create a second admission, but ALREADY ADMITTED requires supervisor review of history and actual physical entry. A green screen alone does not prove that a person crossed the entrance.
6. **Search manually:** enter name or booking reference. Ordinary staff see minimum entrance data. If no match exists, refer to the supervisor; do not improvise a registration or treat a payment screenshot as admission proof.
7. **Manual check-in:** Event Admin/Organizer can select a found eligible ticket and record a reason. It uses the same transaction, duplicate, expiry, capacity and policy checks. It is an alternate lookup, not an override.
8. **Internet/server outage:** stop uncertain admissions. Supervisor establishes one coordinated numbered paper ledger and controlled roster. If reachable, organizer changes online → manual, freezing ticket changes. Otherwise record the transition physically and inform every desk. Never admit independently from multiple stale copies. Record booking reference, unique ledger ID, actual timezone-aware time, staff initials and reason.
9. **Recovery/authority:** organizer changes manual → reconciling, previews/imports the reviewed ledger and resolves unknown/cancelled references. Repeating a committed batch is safe. Resume online only after physical totals and every desk are reconciled. Missing tickets/capacity problems require an organizer decision within approved policy; there is no scanner bypass.
10. **Report an incident:** Incidents → Add record; supply category, severity, short description and owner. Do not include raw QR credentials or unnecessary personal data. Immediate safety incidents follow venue procedures and named emergency contacts, without waiting for the application.

Organizer must supply entrance supervisor/deputy names, venue contacts, desk assignments and approved doors time before opening. They are not invented in this manual.
